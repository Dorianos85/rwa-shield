DATA LINEAGE

Know what you are looking at.

Model assumptions & limitations
  • Reserve pressure, tested collateral and average borrower health are separate models.
  • NAV/reference data may be an explicitly labelled proxy. Issuer NAV and Kamino oracle prices remain distinct.
  • Jupiter quotes measure tested sale sizes, not total DEX TVL. Offline uses the last measurement when available; synthetic curves require explicit selection.
  • The existing ECV engine supplies lending haircuts and breakers. Scenario results are illustrative risk analysis, not historical validation.
  • Unavailable protocol parameters remain unavailable; no generic utilization kink is assumed.
